# AI In Healthcare Notes

Artificial intelligence in healthcare spans diagnostics, workflow automation, revenue cycle support, patient engagement, and clinical decision support. Production use is constrained by model reliability, bias, privacy rules, validation burden, and integration cost.

Common impact areas:

- Diagnostic support in radiology and pathology
- Ambient documentation and clinical summarization
- Triage and patient communication workflows
- Hospital operations forecasting and bed management
- Drug discovery and trial design support

Common validation concerns:

- Source traceability for every clinical claim
- Human oversight for high-risk decisions
- Monitoring model drift and data quality
- Compliance with privacy and safety requirements
